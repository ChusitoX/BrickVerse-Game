# BrickVerse 🧱

> *Remember that this was made with AI.*

BrickVerse is a 3D blocky toy game launcher, world builder studio, and avatar customizer running entirely in your browser using Three.js and React.

## 🎮 Game Experiences (16 Worlds)

1. **Zombie Defense: Night Outpost** — Defend the fortified desert fort from mutant block zombies! Barricade walls, mount watchtowers, and fire rocket launchers.
2. **Paintball CTF: Stadium Clash** — Infiltrate the enemy fortress, snatch their team flag, and sprint home while dodging colorful bouncing paintball volleys!
3. **Rollercoaster Theme Park: Loop Paradise** — Ride the colossal looping coaster across sky tracks, visit carnival food stalls, and launch high in the sky!
4. **Prop Hunt: Warehouse Mystery** — Disguise yourself as a crate, barrel, or locker and freeze in place while seeker bots search every warehouse corridor!
5. **Kart Racing: Neon Grand Prix** — Burn rubber across banked asphalt turns and neon boost chevrons! Race against the clock to set the fastest lap record.
6. **Laser Tag: Cyber Matrix** — Glow-in-the-dark cyber battles in a neon double-decker maze with shield recharge pads and bouncing laser beams!
7. **Obby Jump: Tower of Bricks** — Climb 6 daring obstacle stages into the clouds. Includes rainbow cloud steps, trampolines, and stage checkpoints.
8. **Disaster Survival: Blox Island** — Flee wandering tornadoes and destructible physics structures across an interactive tropical island.
9. **Pizza Chef Tycoon** — Roll fresh dough, spread sauce and pepperoni at the topping bar, bake in the industrial conveyor oven, and deliver orders for BloxCoins.
10. **Sword Arena: Red vs Blue** — Gladiator arena battle against smart knight bots with swords, speed/gravity coils, knockback physics, and respawn cycles.
11. **Speed Run: Neon Galaxy** — High-speed space parkour with neon hyperspeed booster pads and launch catapults.
12. **Mystery Murder Mansion** — Explore a 2-story haunted manor, evade the Impostor Bot, and collect golden keys to unlock the escape vault.
13. **Hide & Seek Extreme: Toy Workshop** — Shrink down into a giant playroom with giant toy blocks, desks, and drums while avoiding the Giant Seeker.
14. **Ragdoll Mayhem: Mega Stair Dismount** — Cannon-launch down a mountain of bouncy stairs, giant bowling pins, and rubber duckies.
15. **The Floor is Lava: Volcano Escape** — Parkour across floating sofas, TVs, and air vents to reach the summit evacuation helipad before magma rises.
16. **World Studio** — Full 3D builder sandbox with color palettes, part scaling, rotation, anchor toggles, and test play.

## 🕹️ Controls

### PC / Desktop Controls
- **WASD / Arrow Keys**: Move character
- **Space / K**: Jump
- **Right-Click Drag / Middle-Click Drag**: Orbit camera 360°
- **Mouse Scroll Wheel**: Zoom camera in / out
- **Left-Click / Q**: Primary Action (Sword slash, paintball shot, drop banana peel, squeak rubber chicken)
- **E**: Interact with tycoon stations, ovens, doors, and clues
- **R**: Ragdoll flop toggle (flop anytime!)
- **1 – 7**: Emote dances (Breakdance, Wave, Backflip, Robot, Floss, Worm, Chicken Dance)
- **Esc**: Pause Menu (settings, resume, return to Discover)
- **Gear Quick-Bar**: Click items at bottom of the screen to equip weapons, coils, and toys

### Mobile & Touch Controls
- **Virtual Analog Joystick**: Smooth 360° movement with radial clamping on bottom-left
- **Touch Camera Swipe**: Swipe anywhere on the right half of the screen to look and steer
- **Two-Finger Pinch**: Pinch in/out to zoom camera
- **⬆️ Jump Button**: Large emerald jump button on bottom-right
- **⚔️ Action Button**: Attack with equipped sword, paintball, or item
- **⚡ Use Button**: Fast interact with tycoon stations and clues
- **🤸 Flop Button**: Instant ragdoll tumble
- **👁️ / 🎥 View Button**: Toggle 1st-person POV and 3rd-person camera
- **🎒 Mobile Gear Drawer**: Tap to open collapsible gear drawer
- **⛶ Fullscreen Button**: Expands to full screen for a native app feel

## 🚀 Deployment to GitHub Pages

1. Push this repository to GitHub.
2. In GitHub, go to **Settings** > **Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment** > **Source**, choose **GitHub Actions**.
4. The included `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` workflow will automatically build the Vite project and deploy it to `https://<username>.github.io/<repo>/`.
